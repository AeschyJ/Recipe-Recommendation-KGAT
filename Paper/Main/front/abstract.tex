\begin{abstract}

隨著網路食譜平台的蓬勃發展，使用者面臨嚴重的資訊過載問題，亟需有效的推薦系統協助探索潛在偏好的食譜內容。本研究以知識圖注意力網路 (Knowledge Graph Attention Network, KGAT) 為核心，在 Food.com 食譜推薦資料集上進行系統性的效能評估與可解釋性研究。

本研究的主要貢獻包含四個面向。首先，透過消融實驗，顯示圖神經網路傳播深度是影響推薦效能的關鍵因素：三層架構 ($L{=}3$) 之 HR@20 達 0.8775，較單層架構提升逾 29\%，較表現最佳之基準模型 LightGCN 提升達 31\%，全面超越 BPR-MF、LightGCN 及 NFM。其次，發現在淺層架構 ($L{=}1$) 下，引入知識圖後觀察到效能下降，與「高連接度節點稀釋協同過濾訊號」之解釋相符，呼應近期文獻~\cite{zhang2024kg4receval}對知識圖效益之檢驗。第三，以均勻隨機抽樣抽選 500 位使用者進行跨模型 Fidelity 量化評估，證實 KGAT-3L 中 94.2\% 有效使用者具正向必要性 ($F^+$)，且直接連接路徑忠實度最高，而語意路徑充分性偏低 ($F^- = 0.2449$)。第四，針對大規模協同知識圖之路徑檢索開銷，本研究結合壓縮稀疏列（CSR）表示法與雙向局部交集搜尋策略，在不改變輸出路徑集合與權重分數的前提下，於本實驗環境中明顯降低單次尋路延遲與記憶體佔用，提升可解釋路徑萃取之執行效率。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}

With the rapid growth of online recipe platforms, users face severe information overload, necessitating effective recommender systems to help discover recipes matching their preferences. This study systematically evaluates the performance and explainability of the Knowledge Graph Attention Network (KGAT) on the Food.com recipe recommendation dataset.

Our contributions are fourfold. First, through ablation experiments, we show that GNN propagation depth is an important factor: the three-layer architecture ($L{=}3$) achieves HR@20 of 0.8775, representing a 29\% improvement over $L{=}1$ and a 31\% improvement over the best-performing baseline LightGCN, substantially outperforming BPR-MF, LightGCN, and NFM. Second, incorporating the knowledge graph in the shallow architecture ($L{=}1$) leads to performance degradation, consistent with high-degree hub nodes diluting collaborative signals---echoing recent work~\cite{zhang2024kg4receval} questioning unconditional KG benefits. Third, uniform random sampling of 500 users for cross-model Fidelity evaluation shows 94.2\% valid users exhibit positive necessity ($F^+$), with direct paths achieving highest fidelity while semantic paths show low sufficiency ($F^- = 0.2449$). Fourth, to reduce the computational overhead of path extraction on large-scale graphs, we implement a bidirectional local intersection search approach based on Compressed Sparse Row (CSR) representations. While maintaining exact equivalence in extracted paths and attention scores, this approach substantially reduces search latency and memory footprint in our experimental environment, facilitating efficient explainability evaluation.

\end{abstract*}
