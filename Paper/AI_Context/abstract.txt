隨著網路食譜平台的蓬勃發展，使用者面臨嚴重的資訊過載問題，亟需有效的推薦系統協助探索潛在偏好的食譜內容。本研究以知識圖注意力網路 (Knowledge Graph Attention Network, KGAT) 為核心，在 Food.com 食譜推薦資料集上進行系統性的效能評估與可解釋性研究。

本研究的主要貢獻包含三個面向。首先，透過消融實驗，顯示圖神經網路的訊息傳播深度是影響推薦效能的重要因素：三層架構 ($L=3$) 的命中率 (Hit Ratio @ 20, HR@20) 達 0.8775，較單層架構 ($L=1$) 提升逾 29%，較最強的基準方法 LightGCN 更提升達 31%，全面超越 BPR-MF、LightGCN 及 NFM 等基準方法。其次，發現在淺層架構 ($L=1$) 下，引入知識圖反而因雜訊稀釋協同過濾訊號而導致效能下降，此發現與近期文獻~所觀察到之知識圖貢獻非必然正面的結論相呼應，為知識圖整合策略提供了重要啟示。最後，為確保評估結果的無偏性 (unbiasedness) 與客觀性，本研究不設任何額外過濾條件，直接以均勻隨機抽樣方式（固定隨機種子 42）從全體使用者中抽選 500 位，對三種不同深度的 KGAT 模型（$L=1$、$L=2$、$L=3$）進行跨模型 忠實度 (Fidelity) 量化評估。在最佳效能模型 KGAT-3L 中，278 位使用者成功萃取解釋路徑（覆蓋率 55.6%），其中 94.2% 的路徑展現正向必要性忠實度 (Fidelity$^+$)，支持注意力權重標記了對預測有貢獻的關鍵邊。進一步的路徑類型分析發現，直接連接路徑的忠實度最高（Avg $F^+ = 0.0494$，$F^- ≈ 0$），而知識圖語意路徑的充分性忠實度持續偏低（$F^- = 0.2449$），暗示深層模型的決策可能已將部分知識圖結構資訊壓縮進分散式嵌入中，使得顯式圖路徑無法完整涵蓋模型的決策依據。

With the rapid growth of online recipe platforms, users face severe information overload, necessitating effective recommender systems to help discover recipes matching their preferences. This study systematically evaluates the performance and explainability of the Knowledge Graph Attention Network (KGAT) on the Food.com recipe recommendation dataset.

Our contributions are threefold. First, through ablation experiments, we show that the message propagation depth of graph neural networks is an important factor for recommendation performance: the three-layer architecture ($L=3$) achieves a Hit Ratio @ 20 (HR@20) of 0.8775, representing a 29% improvement over the single-layer architecture ($L=1$) and a 31% improvement over the strongest baseline LightGCN, substantially outperforming baseline methods including BPR-MF, LightGCN, and NFM. Second, we discover that incorporating the knowledge graph in the shallow architecture ($L=1$) paradoxically degrades performance, as noise dilutes the collaborative filtering signals---a finding consistent with recent work~ questioning the unconditional benefit of knowledge graphs in recommender systems---providing important insights for knowledge graph integration strategies. Third, to ensure the unbiasedness and objectivity of the evaluation results, this study applies no additional filtering conditions and directly selects a cohort of 500 users from the entire user population via uniform random sampling (with a fixed random seed of 42) to conduct a cross-model Fidelity evaluation across three KGAT depths ($L=1$, $L=2$, $L=3$). In the best-performing  KGAT-3L model, 278 users yield explanation paths (coverage 55.6%), of which 94.2% exhibit positive necessity fidelity (Fidelity$^+$), supporting that attention weights identify edges contributing to predictions. Path-type analysis further reveals that direct connection paths achieve the highest fidelity (Avg $F^+ = 0.0494$, $F^- ≈ 0$), while knowledge graph semantic paths exhibit persistently poor sufficiency fidelity ($F^- = 0.2449$), suggesting that deep models may partly compress knowledge graph structural information into distributed representations where explicit graph paths cannot fully capture the model's decision evidence.
